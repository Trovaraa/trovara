What if a farm could restore the land, feed people, create jobs, strengthen nearby communities, and make it clearer where food comes from?

That is what Trovara is trying to build.

Agriculture often feels distant: something that happens somewhere else before food reaches a market stall, a restaurant kitchen, or a dining table.

We think that distance is a problem. So we are building a pilot regenerative food company in Ogun State, Nigeria, to grow food in a way that also serves the communities around the farm.

We start with the basics: healthy soils, responsible farming practices, quality ingredients, and respect for the land.

## What we want to build next

Over time we want agriculture, food, processing, technology, hospitality, and community work to sit in one connected system.

A place where food is grown carefully, turned into products people actually use, sold to families and businesses, and experienced in ways that bring people closer to the land that grew it.

We begin with crops, livestock, and regenerative farming. Later that expands into processed foods, tech tools, education, agritourism, and other ways for people to engage with agriculture.

> Agriculture should produce better food, better livelihoods, and stronger communities, and restore confidence in what we eat, not only higher volume.

Home cooks, chefs, and buyers for hotels or restaurants who care about quality, provenance, and sustainability: follow along from the start.

We are building Trovara for you.
